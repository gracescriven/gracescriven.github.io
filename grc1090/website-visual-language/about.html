Sundaze was born from a love for long beach days, golden sunsets, and that effortless glow that comes from spending a little too much time in the sun. Founded along the California coast by two longtime friends, Emma and Chloe, Sundaze started with a simple idea: what if you could bottle up the feeling of a perfect summer day and carry it with you everywhere?

After countless afternoons spent by the Pacific, the two founders noticed how much they loved the natural, bronzed, dewy look their skin had after a day at the beach. They wanted to create beauty products that captured that same feeling — effortless, radiant, and never overdone. What started as a shared passion quickly turned into a brand built around the beauty of feeling good in your own skin.

Sundaze brings together skincare and makeup designed to give you that fresh, sun-kissed look all year long. From hydrating essentials to glowy skin tints and warm bronzing favorites, every product is inspired by the colors, textures, and warmth of the coast. We believe beauty should feel easy, look natural, and fit seamlessly into your everyday routine.

To us, Sundaze is more than just a beauty brand. It's the feeling of warm sand between your toes, salty hair after a swim, golden light on your skin, and those few moments when the world feels a little slower. Wherever you are, we want you to feel like you're right back at the beach. Stay golden, stay glowing, and live in your Sundaze.
