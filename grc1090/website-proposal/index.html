Introduction

Sundaze

Sundaze is a beauty brand that focuses on providing high-quality, natural skincare and makeup products made to give that post beach day glow that we all crave. The glowy and nourishing formulations are designed to hydrate and revitalize the skin, leaving it looking radiant and sunkissed.

Target audience

This site will be used by people who are interested in natural, high-quality skincare and makeup products that provide a radiant, sunkissed look, like you just got back from a day at the beach.

Their goals on this site are to find the right products to help them feel confident and nourished in their own skin while providing an enhanced glow.
